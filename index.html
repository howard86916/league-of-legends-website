The Game

What is League of Legends?

Released by Riot Games in 2009, League of Legends is a free-to-play MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) and one of the most played PC games in the world. Each match is a fresh start: every player begins at level 1, earns gold, buys items, and grows stronger over roughly 25–35 minutes.

Positions

The Five Roles

On Summoner's Rift, each player on a team usually takes one position.

Top

Durable fighters and tanks who duel on the long top lane, often holding their own in isolation and teleporting into fights.

Jungle

Roams between lanes, clears neutral camps, secures dragons and Baron, and sets up ambushes ("ganks").

Mid

The central lane. Often mages and assassins who deal burst damage and can quickly help either side of the map.

Bot (ADC)

Ranged marksmen who deal steady damage. Weak early but become a team's main damage source late in the game.

Support

Protects the ADC, places wards for vision, and enables teammates with heals, shields and crowd control.

The Map

Summoner's Rift

The main battleground. Three lanes connect the two bases, separated by a jungle full of neutral monsters and a river that cuts the map in half.

  • Turrets defend each lane and deal heavy damage to champions that dive them.
  • Inhibitors: destroying one makes the enemy spawn powerful super minions.
  • Dragons grant permanent team-wide bonuses; four earn the Dragon Soul.
  • Rift Herald & Voidgrubs help teams crack open turrets.
  • Baron Nashor empowers the whole team to push for the win.
